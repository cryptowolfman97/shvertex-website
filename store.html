Step 03
Allow installation
Android will show an "Install blocked" warning. Tap Settings, then enable "Allow from this source", then press Back to continue.
This is a one-time setting per app (e.g. your browser or Files app). It only applies to that specific app installing APKs, not a system-wide change.
